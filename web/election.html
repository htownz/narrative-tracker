      Voting statistics registered voters, early vote, Election Day turnout

      Texas had a record 19,041,684 registered voters. An estimated 7.73 million (40.6%) voted during the 12-day early voting period, a midterm record. The comparable midterm is 2018: 8.37 million voters, 53.0% of 15.8 million registered.

      Early-vote figures are the report's (Texas Secretary of State; Texas Tribune; preliminary). Votes cast and precincts are the SOS election-night county file and update through the night.
