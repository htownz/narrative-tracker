why
DSHS moved the 2026 joint report of the Maternal Mortality and Morbidity Review Committee from its usual Sept. 1 date to Dec. 1. That is after the Nov. 3 election. Ectopic pregnancy deaths rose faster in states with abortion bans than in other states.
factcheckableagainstsupported
why
ProPublica analyzed CDC WONDER data. It found that ectopic deaths rose nationwide, but the climb was much steeper in states that banned abortion after Roe was overturned . For 2023–2025 the rate was 13.1 deaths per million live births in the 18 strict-ban states (bans at six weeks or earlier), against 7.1 in states that never passed a ban . CNN, the Dallas Observer and Oklahoma Watch repeat the finding. The claim is worded as a comparison of rates of increase, with no stated cause, and that matches the data. Caveats: this is a newsroom review of federal data, not a peer-reviewed study . The "ban" group means strict bans only. The ban-state group already had higher mortality in 2014, and both groups jumped in 2020 , before Dobbs. Researchers also cautioned that the data do not prove bans caused the increase . The claim as worded doesn't assert causation, so it is supported. [1] [2] [3] [4]
