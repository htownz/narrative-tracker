factcheckableagainstmisleading
why
The claim mixes two datasets that were built with different methods. The Harvard Law Review (April 2024) counted 6 nationwide injunctions against Bush-era policies and 12 against Obama-era policies. It counted only 14 through the end of Biden's third year . The figure of 28 for Biden comes from a separate Congressional Research Service (CRS) report of March 2025. That report, which used its own methodology, identified 28 for the Biden administration and 86 for the first Trump administration. CRS says there is no single definitive count, and counts vary with the definition used. CRS never applied its method to Bush or Obama. The Department of Justice's February 2020 tally also differs: 12 for Bush and 19 for Obama.
