SNAP users spend $8 billion on DoorDash every year.
factcheckableagainstunsupported
why
I found no record or reporting that SNAP users spend $8 billion a year on DoorDash. The "$8 billion" figure that does appear in SNAP coverage is the monthly total of all SNAP benefits. USDA warned that 40+ million beneficiaries would miss an estimated $8 billion in November benefits , and another source says benefits average about $8 billion per month . The claim may have confused that number with DoorDash. Total SNAP spending was $101.7 billion in FY 2025 . Total online SNAP redemptions across all retailers were $2.3 billion in the July–September 2023 quarter , which is roughly $9 billion a year at that rate. An $8 billion share for DoorDash alone is therefore implausible, and no source supports it. DoorDash accepts EBT only for groceries and convenience orders, and SNAP recipients may only purchase SNAP-approved grocery items with their EBT cards . DoorDash's own September 2026 report gives user growth, from nearly 3 million to more than 4.5 million consumers with SNAP/EBT cards added between June 2025 and June 2026 . It gives no dollar total of $8 billion. [1] [2] [3] [4]

factcheckableagainstsupported
why
The Center on Budget and Policy Priorities (CBPP) SNAP Tracker says SNAP participation fell in Arizona by 52 percent from July 2025 to June 2026 . The same tracker says the decline is 46 percent through August 2026, using newer state data. USDA figures reported by Newsweek show Arizona recorded 465,659 fewer participants in June 2026 than a year earlier. The CBPP tracker's August figure is a drop of 416,924 , which fits "about 400,000". Arizona has the largest percentage decline in the country. Louisiana, the next largest in the Newsweek and CBPP data, fell about 21.5 percent . Children are included: 205,223 children are no longer receiving the benefit since July 2025, a 55% drop , according to AZ Luminaria. The AEI piece notes that about 35% of the reduction comes from shifting costs to states, but it is still a cut to federal SNAP spending, so "cut SNAP" is a fair description. The wording is accurate.
