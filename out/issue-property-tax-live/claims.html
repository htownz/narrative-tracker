Home sales are at a 31-year low and mortgage rates hit 7.24% in mid-September.
factcheckablemisleading
why
The 7.24% figure has a real source, but I found nothing supporting "31-year low" for home sales. Fortune reported the daily 30-year fixed mortgage rate rose to 7.24% in the week before its Sept. 22 article. Mortgage News Daily's index also shows 7.24% on 9/16/2026 . The benchmark most often cited as "the mortgage rate" says otherwise. Freddie Mac's weekly average was 6.95% as of September 17, up from 6.76% the week before. It did not reach 7.03% until September 24. So 7.24% is a daily lender-quote index reading, not the Freddie Mac average. On sales, NAR reported existing-home sales fell 2% in August to a 3.98 million annual rate, the lowest since June 2025. That is a roughly 14-month low, not a 31-year low. NAR's Yun also noted that sales over the first eight months of 2026 were up 1.6% from the same period in 2025. The "lowest in about 30 years" framing comes from annual totals: 2024 was the weakest year since 1995 , and 2025 annual sales remained at a 30-year low. I found no NAR or other reporting calling any 2026 reading a 31-year low. The claim therefore appears to stretch earlier annual lows into a current one. Property taxes make Texas unaffordable 1
Texas under Greg Abbott has higher taxes and more data centers.
factcheckableagainstmisleading
why
- Taxes: Total Texas property tax levies did rise, from $73.5 billion in 2021 to $89.4 billion in 2025, a 22 percent jump according to the Texas Public Policy Research dashboard. That dashboard uses Comptroller data. Abbott's own site concedes that many Texans did not feel his reforms because local jurisdictions raised their taxes even more . But one analysis of Comptroller figures found the levy per $100 of market value fell from about $1.95 in 2014 to about $1.58 in 2025 . Compared with other states, the Tax Foundation ranks Texas #6 lowest at 8.6% for total state-and-local burden. WalletHub puts its property tax burden 9th highest but its overall burden 36th. "Higher taxes" is therefore true for dollar levies over time and for property taxes, but not for the overall burden or for rates. - Data centers: Counts depend on the tracker. Pew, using Data Center Map figures, counted 398 operating data centers in Virginia and 296 in Texas in early 2026. Other trackers put Texas first on total listings or pipeline, for example 898 tracked projects at PoweredByWho. Texas has clearly added many, but "more" is true only against its own past or against most states. It is not true [1] [2] [3] [4]

Abbott's promise is election-year spin 1

factcheckablesupported
why
The claim matches Abbott's stated position. At a Pasadena campaign event he said the local school district portion of a homeowner's bill "should be zero dollars" ( Click2Houston, Oct. 7, 2026 ). His five-point plan's last item is a constitutional amendment asking voters whether to eliminate school district property taxes for homeowners (KWTX). His press secretary told CBS News Texas that Abbott wants policy that "eliminates the school district property tax for homeowners" . Two details qualify the claim without making it inaccurate. The elimination applies to homesteads, so it covers homeowners and not renters or businesses. It would also need voter approval of a constitutional amendment, and Abbott estimated state funding would replace the lost revenue at about $10 billion .
