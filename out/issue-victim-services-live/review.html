The victim was a young boy who was a friend of Hoffman’s son. According to the boy’s statements to investigators and testimony, the abuse occurred over roughly three years (around 2019–2022), beginning when he was about 6–7 years old (third grade). Key terms of the initial offer: - Guilty plea to the misdemeanors. - Jail sentence of time served plus one day (Hoffman had already spent one day in custody after arrest). - No requirement to register as a sex offender (because the victim’s age was not included in the plea documents). - Later additions discussed included a lifetime protective order barring contact with the victim.
