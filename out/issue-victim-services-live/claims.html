Adam Hoffman committed a harmful act against a child.
factcheckableagainstsupported
why
Adam Dean Hoffman, a former Waco attorney, pleaded guilty on April 16 to indecent assault and displaying harmful materials to a minor, both Class A misdemeanors (KWTX). That is an admission in court of harmful conduct involving a child, so the claim as worded has a solid basis. The original charge was continuous sexual abuse of a young child. That trial ended in a mistrial in June 2025 after jurors deadlocked 7-5 in favor of guilt , so he was never convicted of the felony. The pleas came through a deal with the Attorney General's office, and a visiting judge then sentenced him to 60 days in jail, doubling the 30-day plea deal . The Texas Tribune and KWTX reporting, along with the Newsweek and Spectrum News coverage, describe the victim as a young boy who was a friend of Hoffman's son. The claim is worded generally, and the guilty pleas back it up. A stronger claim, such as a felony conviction or rape, would not be supported by the court outcome. [1] [2] [3]
