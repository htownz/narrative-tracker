The forecast storms had not yet arrived in Texas and are coming later today.
factcheckablemisleading
why
The claim has no time of day, region or author, so it can only be checked against conditions on 1 October 2026, and on that day it mostly doesn't hold. CBS Texas's live blog shows the National Weather Service tracking strong thunderstorms in North Texas at 2:58 a.m. on Oct. 1. The Dallas Morning News reported the first widespread measurable rain in D-FW since mid-July , with flash flood warnings and high water on Interstate 30 in Fort Worth that morning. MySA reported FAA ground stops and delays at San Antonio International Airport on Thursday, October 1, as storms crossed Texas . The claim is only accurate for some areas and times. KLTV's East Texas forecast on Oct. 1 said heavy rains and thunderstorms would arrive later that day , and the WPC outlook for Oct. 1–2 still covered storms to come along the I-35 corridor. So the "coming later today" part is true for East Texas and for later rounds elsewhere. But "had not yet arrived in Texas" is wrong as a statewide statement for Oct. 1, and it could only be right for Sept. 30 or a regional, early-morning snapshot. [1] [2] [3] [4]
