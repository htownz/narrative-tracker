factcheckableforsupported
after: YouGov Texas poll: Talarico 50-44, Hinojosa 48-47
why
UMass Lowell's Center for Public Opinion lists a UMass Lowell/YouGov poll of 850 likely Texas voters. It was conducted Sept. 18 to 28, with an adjusted margin of error of 4.5%. Political Wire reported that the poll finds Abbott (R) leading Hinojosa (D) 48% to 44%, with Pat Dixon (L) at 1%. Newsweek gave the same figures and added that Abbott's lead is within the margin of error. Later Newsweek and Yahoo/Austin-area poll roundups still list the UMass Lowell result as Abbott 48%, Hinojosa 44%. The claim's numbers, pollster, and population (likely voters) all match. The poll was published around Oct. 8, two days after the claim was first seen on X (Oct. 6). The claim doesn't state a release date, so I did not treat that as an error. I could not open UMass Lowell's own release, only its poll index page. The figures come from Newsweek and Political Wire. Other polls cited in the same coverage show a closer race, and some show Hinojosa ahead. That doesn't change the accuracy of this claim. [1] [2] [3] [4]
