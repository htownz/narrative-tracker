Texas Narrative Trackers · weekly brief · Oct 12 – Oct 19, 2026

In the Senate race, attacks on James Talarico took over from the story of his absence and the CBS News/YouGov poll on Oct. 18 produced the week's largest spike, 115.8 times the prior 12 hours, as early voting opened Oct. 19.

Views on the Senate race fell 22% to 143.1 million, but the lane calling Talarico radical, weird or socialist rose 247% to 73.3 million, half of all views. The Talarico absence story fell 93%. Two Talarico claims were rated unsupported or misleading, the midnight texting claim and the claim that his campaign sat on evidence, and a supported claim about his past 'God is non-binary' remarks reached 15.3 million views. In the governor's race, views rose 65% to 20.1 million, Abbott's Kalshi price fell from 81 to 76 cents, and Abbott's no-show at Hinojosa's debate event was verified. Vance's Tyler rally pushed Vance in Texas up from 2.4 million to 18.8 million views, and the data center debate went from 2.6 million to 33.2 million views on one post by @elonmusk.

The Senate race

Views fell to 143.1 million from 182.3 million the week before, down 22%. Posts fell to 2,907 from 3,206. The mix changed more than the total. The lane casting Talarico as radical, weird or socialist drew 73.3 million views, 51% of the total, up 247%. The polls-and-odds lane drew 33.2 million, 23%, up 40%. Paxton corruption and personal scandals drew 12.8 million, 9%, up 195%. Pushback fell 62% to 14.2 million, 10%. The lane on Talarico's absence and the flu 'cover story' fell 93% to 5.0 million. Paxton ducking debates fell 75% to 3.2 million. The leaked-audio lane fell 85% to 1.4 million.

The largest claim was the opinion 'James Talarico hates Texans,' at 31.5 million views this week across 5 posts. It was first seen Sept. 1 from @HouseofElon. Two new fact claims came from @KanekoaTheGreat on Oct. 18. One was that Talarico has said 'God is non-binary,' that there are six biological sexes, and that women are 'neighbors with a uterus.' It was rated supported. All three remarks are documented, and Snopes dates the 'God is non-binary' remark to Oct. 14, 2021, during debate on HB 25. The other was that early voting starts tomorrow, also rated supported. The Secretary of State calendar lists Oct. 19 to Oct. 30. Both claims carry 15.3 million views. The account's own views were 15.3 million, with 31.3 million more downstream.

Four claims were rated against the poster. 'Talarico said parents who do not transition their kids are abusive' was rated misleading. It reached 15.3 million views this week and 24.5 million in total, and was first seen Sept. 14 from @WellsJorda89710. The source is a 2019 hearing on HB 1910, where he said denying a child's gender identity is 'incredibly harmful' and that 'whether or not we label it with the term abuse, it's abusive for sure.' 'Talarico texted with former female middle school students at midnight and urged them to contact him privately' was rated unsupported, at 4.2 million views. It originated with @libsoftiktok on Oct. 15, and no record shows the specific allegations. 'Talarico's campaign sat on evidence while the hospital rumor spread' was rated misleading, at 3.2 million views from @foxnewspolitics. The New York Times reviewed timestamped home-security images the campaign supplied, on condition they not be published. The claim that the disappearance story was invented by MAGA accounts was rated misleading. The Ohio psychiatric-hospital rumor is a fabrication: Snopes rated it False, PolitiFact Pants on Fire, and the Lindner Center of Hope said he was never a patient. The Kalshi claim of a 63% Talarico chance, from @KalshiPolitics, was rated misleading. Markets showed 50-50 on Sept. 5 and 54% for Talarico on Sept. 15.

The polling claim was verified. A Quantus likely-voter poll showing Paxton 47.2% and Talarico 46.7% was rated supported, at 4.5 million views across 24 posts, first from @PollTracker2024. The fieldwork ran Oct. 9 to 11, with 854 respondents and a 3.6-point margin of error. Trump's Truth Social post calling Talarico 'TalaFREAKO' and a 'Radical Left Lunatic and Vegan,' and endorsing Paxton, drew 4.0 million views. Views in the 12 hours after it ran 3.4 times the prior 12. The Insider Advantage poll on Oct. 15 ran 4.0 times, and the CBS News/YouGov poll on Oct. 18 ran 115.8 times. The top lane after both of those polls was Talarico as radical, weird or socialist, not the polls lane. Other top accounts: @elonmusk, one post, 31.3 million views; @PpollingNumbers, 13 posts, 4.5 million; @PollTracker2024, 40 posts, 3.3 million; @KalshiPolitics, 13 posts, 2.4 million.

Officials posted into the Talarico-as-radical lane. Paxton's ad said Talarico 'wants to set our way of life ablaze' and 'declared war on Texas oil and Texas beef' (171,322 views). A second ad said he 'has never met a tax increase he didn't love' (56,680). Paxton also posted a Ben Ferguson clip asking whether Texans want children 'mutilated' or men in women's sports (30,455). Ted Cruz posted, 'If Texans wanted Obama, they would've voted for him' (70,245). The Texas Democratic Party posted that Paxton 'works for his donors' (3,430), in the corruption lane. The tracker flagged four link rings sharing coverage of Paxton's Dubai trip, from the New York Times, New Republic and Wall Street Journal, with 18 to 34 accounts each and 4,517 to 40,088 views. The tracker flagged them as suspicious. Alerts flagged new clusters on the Chamber of Commerce backing Paxton and an early voting push. Paxton's Kalshi price moved from 37 to 39 cents, in a range of 36 to 40.

Trump's Texas footprint

Views fell 25% to 101.3 million from 134.8 million, and posts fell to 1,633 from 2,030. The Talarico freak, vegan and missing lane led with 33.2 million views, 33%, down 26%. The lane on polls showing a tight race rose 116% to 16.6 million, 16%. The lane on Paxton scandals and Trump's choice rose 22% to 14.9 million, 15%. Pushback fell 50%, 'Trump dragging down Texas GOP' fell 60% to 7.2 million, and the Trump rally lane fell 36% to 5.4 million.

The largest claim was supported. @KristiNoem posted on Oct. 12 'I'll take Ken Paxton over James Talarico,' and it drew 10.1 million views from one post. The same post is the top item in the border tracker. Three claims from @libsoftiktok on Oct. 15 reached 4.2 million views each. 'Talarico texted with former female middle school students at midnight' was rated misleading here. The account's May 29 post described screenshots with one former student and no mention of midnight. 'Talarico repeatedly urged students to text and call him privately' was rated misleading. Old Facebook posts from his time as a sixth-grade teacher included his cell number, but the claim goes further than they do. 'Walking red flag' is opinion.

Two more claims were rated misleading. 'Talarico's campaign withheld evidence while the hospital rumor spread' (3.2 million, @foxnewspolitics) was rated misleading. The New York Times reviewed images the campaign supplied showing him at home Oct. 1 to Oct. 8. The claim that racism is a virus white people spread by their skin color (886K, @FoxNews) was also rated misleading. He did tweet about a 'virus of racism' in May 2020, but the claim drops the context of the tweets. Kalshi's 63% Talarico claim was rated misleading too, because the recap that gave 63% was dated Oct. 9 and the market had moved to 64% by Oct. 12. Trump's Oct. 17 post called Talarico a 'Radical Left Lunatic and Vegan' and drew 3.99 million views. @StephenM drew 6.3 million views across 6 posts.

Paxton posted 'We don't have months anymore. We have days. If we lose Texas, we lose America' (244,044 views, rally-loyalty lane). Talarico posted that Paxton 'showed up in London on the 4th of July to party' and in Dubai, and that a Senate candidate should 'show up for the job interview' (340,559, scandals lane). The tracker flagged link rings on the Wall Street Journal Paxton-Dubai story (16 accounts), ProPublica's Abbott border story (23), the Guardian on Fort Hood (18), and Raw Story (14), with 0 to 1,146 views each.

The governor's race

Views rose 65% to 20.1 million from 12.1 million, and posts rose to 820 from 554. The 'Abbott in trouble, Hinojosa surging' lane drew 10.5 million views, 52%, up 81%. 'Abbott is solid and will win' drew 4.8 million, 24%, up 66%. Pushback rose 374% to 2.0 million. Abbott's Kalshi price fell from 81 to 76 cents, in a range of 74 to 82.

The poll claims were mostly verified. The claim that CBS/YouGov shows Abbott ahead 52% to 48% was rated supported, at 2.1 million views, first from @PollTracker2024. Fieldwork ran Oct. 8 to 16 with 1,458 likely voters. The claim that UT/Texas Politics Project shows Hinojosa ahead 49% to 48% was rated supported (1.25 million, @jamesrhenson). Views in the 12 hours after the CBS poll ran 15.0 times the prior 12. Views after Hinojosa's debate challenge at the Governor's Mansion ran 12.1 times, and after the GBAO poll 13.7 times. 'Abbott did not show up to a debate with Hinojosa' was rated supported (937K). Fox 7 Austin reported an empty podium. The claim that Paxton said in recorded audio that 'Abbott is struggling' was rated supported (1.3 million, 18 posts, first from @RollingStone). The audio is from a Sept. 24 private fundraiser in Washington.

Five claims were rated misleading. The Harry Enten claim (456K) was rated misleading: he put Talarico's chance at 70% on Oct. 4 for the Senate race only. The ReconMR/Siena 48-47 Abbott claim (373K) was rated misleading because it comes from a later poll than the one circulating when the claim was first seen. The claim that Hinojosa had write-in candidate Jenn Mack removed from the stage (262K) was rated misleading: security escorted Mack off after Hinojosa said the event was meant to be a debate with Abbott. 'Hinojosa has never voted to raise Texans' property taxes' (231K) was rated misleading: the Houston Chronicle found she never directly voted on property taxes, since the state levies none. The claim that Texas is considering selling a state park to Elon Musk (230K, @Forrest4Trees) was rated misleading. Texas Monthly reports only that the Parks and Wildlife Commission has discussed selling Boca Chica State Park, with a closed-door session listed for Nov. 5.

Officials posted. Hinojosa's 'Jesse knows it's time for change' post drew 2.4 million views. She posted 'I've never voted to raise taxes. Full stop.' (195,556) and said Abbott 'has the power to issue a moratorium and call the legislature into special session today' on data centers (11,883). The Texas Democratic Party posted that Musk donated $500K to Abbott and got a $2 billion tax break (64,687). The Republican Party of Texas posted on Abbott's turnout message at a luncheon (1,228). @GinaHinojosaTX posted 16 times for 3.2 million views. The tracker flagged link rings on ProPublica's Abbott border story (24 accounts) and on the UT poll blog (8 accounts, 493,910 views).

Vance in Texas

Views rose to 18.8 million from 2.4 million, and posts to 569 from 29. Vance's Oct. 13 rally for Paxton in Tyler drove it. Views in the 12 hours after the rally ran 25.3 times the prior 12. The lane calling Vance's insinuations smears drew 6.5 million views (34%), 'Vance mocks Talarico as freak' 5.9 million (32%, up 615%), and 'Vance touts Texas administration wins' 2.6 million (14%).

Three claims about the rally were rated supported. 'Vance told a man who said he loved him that he is the vice president, not James Talarico' drew 3.7 million views across 25 posts, first from @atrupar. He said 'I love you too, sir... in a totally platonic way, to be clear.' 'Vance implied Talarico is gay' drew 1.4 million (@atrupar.com). The claim that Vance announced use of the Defense Production Act to expand the Delek refinery in Tyler drew 2.4 million (first from @bwaltens). Opinion that the move will lower fuel prices drew 1.7 million (@EricLDaugh).

Five claims were rated against the poster. 'Vance baselessly suggested Talarico is a pedophile' (252K) was rated misleading: Vance never used the word, but said people who say things like Talarico's 'tend to have some unusual search histories.' 'Vance joked 'Not a Homo!'' (216K) was rated misleading because the words are not his. The racism-virus claim from @EricLDaugh (932K) was rated misleading. 'The Talarico campaign has not explained where he was for two weeks' (538K, @StephenM) was rated unsupported, since the campaign said he had the flu. A claim about the crowd size at the rally (407K) was rated misleading. @atrupar led with 2.5 million own views and 3.9 million downstream.

Vance's office posted the refinery announcement (145,437). Paxton thanked Vance and said '21 days until Election Day' (63,026). Speaker Dustin Burrows posted that East Texas 'was fired up' (1,367 and 4,798). The tracker flagged link rings on Raw Story (18, 10 and 9 accounts), with 0 views, and on The Hill's Defense Production Act story (7 accounts, 6,627 views).

The claim that a UT poll has Vikki Goodwin ahead of Dan Patrick 49% to 46% was rated supported, at 984K views this week. @PollTracker2024 first posted it Sept. 17. Views in the 12 hours after the UT/Texas Politics Project poll ran 4.4 times. Patrick's Kalshi price was 73 cents at both ends of the week, in a range of 66 to 74.

Patrick posted that Goodwin wants a '50% gas tax hike' (4,350) and urged Republicans to vote early (1,671 and 2,031). Issues

Data centers: views rose to 33.2 million from 2.6 million, and 92% came through the pushback lane. One post by @elonmusk, 'James Talarico hates Texas,' drew 29.4 million of them (first seen Oct. 18). It is opinion. The claim that BlackRock's Larry Fink said a moratorium could trigger a recession was rated supported (867K, @unusual_whales). CNBC reported on Oct. 14 that he spoke of a national moratorium. Talarico's side said data centers fund Paxton's campaign, rated supported (441K): NOTUS found at least $448,000 through June from data center executives and committees. A repeat of that figure was rated misleading, because it covers several committees. 'Paxton gave data centers billions in special tax breaks' was rated unsupported. The exemption costs $3.2 billion over two years, but the claim ties it to Paxton. Paxton posted that he sent letters to data center developments on water-use reporting (26,324). Hinojosa called for a moratorium.

The border: views rose to 10.5 million from 0.5 million. 99% came from the Noem post preferring Paxton (10.1 million). @BPUnion's two claims on Talarico and border funding were each rated misleading (108K). The $800 million figure traces to a 2019 interview, and he has since said Biden 'failed us' on the border. ProPublica's report that Abbott has renewed his 2021 border emergency proclamation at least 69 times drew 63K views and has no verdict. Hinojosa posted from Eagle Pass (25,351).

Election integrity: views fell 28% to 7.5 million. The martial-law lane drew 5.7 million, 76%. The claim that Trump allies are discussing martial law or taking control of the midterms was rated supported (5.4 million, 30 posts). It cites the Atlantic and Axios. The @EricLDaugh claim that nearly one in five Tulare voters got two ballots was rated misleading (312K): 7,791 voters in the city of Tulare, which has more than 34,000 registered. The Harris County poll with Talarico leading 58 to 36 was rated supported (285K, @PatrickSvitek). Claims that Musk rigged the 2024 election were rated unsupported, and the Tina Peters and Time interview claims misleading.

Autonomous vehicles: views fell 75% to 5.3 million. Most of it was Tesla's own account (2.4 million). 'Tesla Cybercabs had zero crashes in their first two weeks' was rated misleading, because the NHTSA data covers 11 days. 'Tesla's Cybercab fleet grew eightfold to 300+' was rated misleading because only Tesla confirms it. Two claims about Cybercab additions and wait times were rated unsupported. D.C.'s presumptive mayor Janeese Lewis George urged delaying a Waymo bill, citing AI job loss (474,545).

Housing: views rose to 4.3 million from 0.4 million. One post on a 'psychotic homeless woman' (3.1 million, @kevinvdahlgren) drove the encampment lane, and was rated unverifiable with no Texas link. The Texas Democratic Party's 'Republicans have governed Texas for 30 years' (27,632) was rated misleading. The governorship has been Republican nearly 32 years. 'Foreign buyers are the reason homes are so expensive' was rated unsupported, since foreign buyers made about 1.7% of purchases.

Other issues: property tax views fell 59% to 739K, led by Paxton's claim that he blocked cities from raising property taxes (184K, rated supported, 130+ cities). Vouchers rose to 423K, with Cruz and Sen. Mark Kelly trading posts. Both Cruz claims were rated misleading, and his claim that Kelly repeats union talking points was rated unsupported. Oil rose to 2.8 million, led by Houthi threats against Aramco and the IEA's diesel outlook, both rated supported. A @bofrench claim about Jon Rosenthal was rated misleading. Houston local government fell 97% to 928K, with the claim that Talarico may be in Hidalgo's psychiatric facility rated unsupported. Hemp fell 98% to 42K. The grid (146K) and 765-kV lines (16K) were quiet. Water was 78K, from one unverified Oct. 18 post about missing Camp Mystic girls and a flood death toll of 13. It has no verdict. Replies said the camp is closed.
