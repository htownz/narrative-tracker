Texas Narrative Trackers · weekly brief · Oct 08 – Oct 15, 2026

A rumor that James Talarico spent his two-week absence at an Ohio psychiatric facility, which the Lindner Center of Hope denied on Oct. 11, drove X views in the Senate race up 58% to 178 million.

Views in the Senate race rose to 178.1 million from 112.4 million the week before. The largest lane was the claim that Talarico was missing and that the flu was a cover story, at 61.4 million views. The Lindner Center of Hope said Oct. 11 that Talarico "has never received treatment" there, and the claims that he was a patient or was discharged were rated unsupported. The Lindner Center's own post drew 10.1 million views, among the largest single posts in the Senate race and in Trump's Texas footprint. Vance's Tyler rally on Oct. 13 lifted Vance in Texas to 14.2 million views from 2.1 million. Polls showed a close race at the top and in the governor's and lieutenant governor's races, and Kalshi had Paxton up to 39 cents from 36.

The Senate race

Views rose 58% to 178.1 million, from 112.4 million. Posts rose to 2,523 from 1,843. The "Talarico missing, flu cover story" lane took 34% of views, or 61.4 million, up 268%. The Pushback lane reached 44.6 million, or 25% of views, up 315%. The lane on polls, odds and who is winning fell 31% to 24.8 million. The lane on Talarico as radical, weird or socialist fell 30% to 20.5 million.

The biggest claims were about the absence. "Talarico was in a mental health facility, the Lindner Center" drew 11.1 million views and was rated unsupported. @spencerpratt first posted it on Oct. 9. The Lindner Center posted that Talarico "has never received treatment from Lindner Center of Hope," and Talarico reshared it and wrote that he has "never even been to Ohio." The claim that a Lindner employee said Talarico was recently discharged drew 9.3 million views and was rated unsupported. @nicksortor first posted it on Oct. 11 with an audio recording of a woman who said she was at the facility. "Talarico lied about having the flu" drew 10.4 million views and was rated unsupported. @KamVTV first posted it on Oct. 8. CNN and the Texas Tribune reported the flu account.

The debunk was also among the largest claims. "The Lindner story is false" drew 13.7 million views and was rated supported. @PNWPragmatist first posted it on Oct. 9. @LindnerCtrHope had 10.1 million own views from one post, plus 5.7 million downstream. @nicksortor had 12.2 million own views across 7 posts. @spencerpratt had 9.7 million across 5 posts. @jamestalarico had 8.3 million across 6 posts.

Two other claims were rated misleading. "The campaign has stopped actively planning events" drew 6.5 million views from a single post by @EdMorrissey. Talarico did stop appearing in person for about 12 days, and his last in-person Texas event was Sept. 26. At least two events were scrapped. "Talarico challenged Paxton to a debate 85 days ago" drew 5.1 million views, first posted by @DemocraticWins. Talarico used the 85-day figure himself, so the rating of misleading does not rest on a made-up number. A third claim, that Talarico said only a child can determine a child's gender identity, drew 8.0 million views and was rated unverifiable. @LoneStar_PAC first posted it on Oct. 11. The Daily Wire quotes the line from a Texas House committee hearing.

Poll news held the polls lane in second place among the Paxton-side themes. The Quantus poll (Paxton 47.2, Talarico 46.7) and the UT/Texas Politics Project poll each pushed views 1.7 to 1.8 times higher over the following 12 hours. Kalshi's Paxton-wins market moved to 39 cents from 36. A new cluster on Musk funding Paxton through SLF was flagged.

Trump's Texas footprint

Views rose 119% to 120.4 million, from 55.0 million. Posts rose to 1,020 from 929. The "Talarico freak, vegan, missing" lane took 42% of views, or 50.8 million, up 383%. Pushback reached 30.7 million, up 986%. The lane on Paxton scandals and Trump's choice rose 211% to 13.6 million and was flagged as a surge.

The Lindner rumor dominated here too. "Talarico was a patient at Lindner, not sick with the flu" drew 17.6 million views and was rated unsupported. @spencerpratt first posted it on Oct. 9. "Talarico lied about the flu; his absence was a serious mental health matter" drew 10.7 million and was rated unsupported. The debunk drew 12.9 million views and was rated supported, first posted by @krassenstein on Oct. 11.

Two claims were rated misleading. "A private plane flew from Hamilton, Ohio to Austin around Talarico's reappearance" drew 3.4 million views, first posted by @CarolineWren. The Dallas Morning News reported the flight path from her screenshots, so the flight detail has support. "The campaign withheld evidence while the rumor spread" drew 2.9 million views, first posted by @foxnewspolitics. The New York Times reviewed campaign home-security images showing him at home Oct. 1 to Oct. 8.

@KristiNoem's Oct. 12 post, "I'll take Ken Paxton over James Talarico," drew 9.9 million views from one post. It was rated supported as a statement of her preference. @nicksortor had 9.8 million own views and @spencerpratt 9.1 million. "Ken Paxton is afraid to debate Talarico" drew 4.9 million views and is an opinion with no verdict.

Vance in Texas

Views rose 565% to 14.2 million, from 2.1 million. Posts rose to 274 from 9. Vance's rally for Paxton in Tyler on Oct. 13 lifted views 23.3 times in the following 12 hours. Pushback was 3% of views. The lane on Vance mocking Talarico as a freak had 5.1 million views. The lane on Vance's insinuations being called smears had 4.0 million.

"Vance told a man at the rally he is the vice president, not James Talarico" drew 3.4 million views and was rated supported. @atrupar first posted it and had 2.4 million own views plus 3.4 million downstream. "Vance announced the Defense Production Act will expand a Tyler refinery" drew 2.0 million views and was rated supported. @bwaltens first posted it. @EricLDaugh posted the opinion that the move lowers fuel prices, which drew 1.3 million views.

Three claims were flagged. "Talarico said racism is a virus white people spread by their skin color" drew 920K views and was rated misleading. Talarico did tweet on this topic on May 8, 2020, but the claim leaves out that he wrote that all white Americans spread it "through our words, our actions, and our systems." "Vance baselessly suggested Talarico is a pedophile" drew 246K views and was rated misleading. Vance did make an insinuation about search history but never used the word. "The campaign has not explained where Talarico was" drew 531K views, first posted by @StephenM, and was rated unsupported. The campaign gave an explanation and security images to the New York Times.

The governor's race

Views fell 41% to 10.3 million, from 17.4 million. Posts fell to 474 from 532. The "Abbott in trouble, Hinojosa surging" lane held 52% of views, or 5.3 million. Kalshi's Abbott-wins market ended at 76 cents, where it began, after trading between 75 and 82.

The UT/Texas Politics Project poll showing Hinojosa ahead 49% to 48% drew 1.1 million views and was rated supported. @jamesrhenson first posted it. The UH Hobby School poll raised views 3.1 times over 12 hours, and the UT poll raised them 1.6 times.

Rolling Stone's Paxton audio drove two claims, both rated supported. "Paxton said Abbott is struggling" drew 925K views. "Paxton conceded Republicans will likely lose seats in the legislature" drew 614K. @RollingStone first posted both.

@JustinVos posted a personal forecast of Abbott 50.3% and Hinojosa 44.0%, which drew 751K views. The claim was rated supported as an accurate description of the post. A viral homeowner video telling an Abbott canvasser "absolutely not" drew 463K views and was rated misleading. The exchange happened in June 2022 during the O'Rourke race, not the 2026 race. New clusters were flagged on Hinojosa ousting independent Jenn Mack and on Abbott ducking a debate.

The lieutenant governor's race

Views fell 46% to 1.5 million, from 2.9 million. The "Patrick vulnerable: polls and odds" lane held 73% of views, or 1.1 million.

The UT poll showing Goodwin ahead of Patrick 49% to 46% drew 931K views this week and was rated supported. @PollTracker2024 first posted it. The poll raised views 4.4 times in the following 12 hours. Kalshi's Patrick-wins market rose to 73 cents from 68.

@scottbraddock posted three unrated claims about Patrick's mental health history and Democratic messaging, each drawing about 33K to 36K views.

Issues

Data centers: Views rose 10% to 1.9 million, from 1.8 million. A new lane on Paxton versus Talarico over data center money rose to 893K views, 46% of the total. @jamestalarico's post drew 427K views and originated the lane. "Data centers fund Paxton and receive tax breaks" was rated supported. NOTUS found at least $448,000 from data center executives and committees through June. "About $450,000 to half a million dollars" was rated misleading. "Paxton gave data centers billions in tax breaks" was rated unsupported. The exemption is real and costs $3.2 billion over two years, per the comptroller. @MediumBuying posted both.

Property tax: Views rose 111% to 1.5 million, from 734K. @unusual_whales drew 892K views from one post with two claims on the national housing market, both rated misleading. The "least affordable on record" claim is partly supported by Redfin figures ($109,800 needed versus $87,599 household income). The "31-year low" claim for home sales found no support. "Paxton as AG blocked cities from raising property taxes" drew 106K views and was rated supported. @KenPaxtonTX first posted it.

Grid: Views fell 46% to 313K. California's governor's office drew 65K views comparing its grid to Texas.

Vouchers: Views rose 56% to 193K. @SenMarkKelly drew 77K views against a national voucher program, and @SenTedCruz drew 56K views in response.

765-kV lines: Views were 17K across 5 posts. @bradj_TX drew 5K views for Brad Buckley's House District 54 ad opposing the lines.

What to watch

  • Election day is Nov 3. The Lindner Center rumor and its debunk were the two biggest claims in the Senate race and Trump's Texas footprint, and the Pushback lane there rose 315% and 986%.
  • Kalshi had Paxton at 39 cents, up from 36, after the polls. The Quantus and UT polls each lifted Senate views 1.7 to 1.8 times.
  • The Musk-SLF cluster is new in the Senate race, and the Paxton scandals and Trump's choice lane surged 211% in Trump's Texas footprint. Both could grow.
  • Rolling Stone's Paxton audio on Abbott struggling and on losing legislative seats. The governor's race fell 41% in views, but the UT poll (Hinojosa 49, Abbott 48) and Hinojosa's debate attack on Abbott are active.
  • Paxton and data center money is the fastest-rising issue lane, up 2,522%. It began with @jamestalarico's Oct. 12 post and runs on NOTUS's $448,000 figure.
