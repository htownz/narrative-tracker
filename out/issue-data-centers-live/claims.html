Grid queue, speculation and pause 4
—
Texas has frozen new data center permits and grid hookups of 75 MW or more, and paused its big grid study.
factcheckablesupported
why
The claim is largely accurate. On Sept. 21, 2026, Gov. Abbott ordered a halt to all data center permits sought from the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality until the PUC and ERCOT finish the audit he started in early August (Sidley; CNBC). ERCOT's September Batch Zero update says it issued a Market Notice on Aug. 3 pausing the Batch Zero study process and paused approvals to energize data centers or crypto facilities of 75 MW or more . Batch Zero is ERCOT's large-load interconnection study, so "big grid study" fits. Reporting adds that the pause covers only grid connections for data centers requesting at least 75 MW, and excludes smaller projects and those building their own power plants (Houston Public Media). Two caveats: the 75 MW threshold belongs to the grid-hookup pause, not the permit freeze, which covers all data center permits. Both measures are pauses by gubernatorial directive pending the audit, and ERCOT's reports are due Dec. 10, 2026. The wording "frozen" and "paused" fits that, but it is not a permanent ban.
